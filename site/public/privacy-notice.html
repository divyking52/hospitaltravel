Who approves this notice

This Privacy Notice is issued and maintained by Smart Strategic Marketing, LLC, the operator of HospitalityCancun.com. HospitalityCancun.com is an information and referral service; it is not a medical provider and is not a division of Grupo Médico Costamed.

Where this notice appears

A link to this Privacy Notice appears:

  • Next to the inquiry form consent checkbox on the homepage contact section
  • In the website footer under Navigation

Who receives the information

When you submit an inquiry and check the authorization box:

  • Smart Strategic Marketing, LLC receives the contact and inquiry details you provide (such as name, email, phone/WhatsApp, location, procedure interest, timeline, and preferred contact method).
  • With your authorization, SMART shares the contact details needed for that inquiry with Grupo Médico Costamed so its team can follow up about Costamed care options.

Submitting the form does not confirm a clinical appointment and is not a medical diagnosis.

Why information is shared

Information is shared only to help you receive a follow-up about medical-travel information related to Grupo Médico Costamed services. SMART does not sell your inquiry information.

How long information is kept

SMART keeps inquiry records for as long as needed to process your request and related follow-up, and for a reasonable business period afterward (generally up to 24 months from the date of your inquiry), unless a longer period is required for legal, security, or dispute-resolution reasons. When retention is no longer necessary, records are deleted or de-identified.

How to contact SMART about privacy

For privacy questions, access requests, or to ask that SMART stop sharing or delete inquiry information it controls, contact:

Please note that once information has been shared with Grupo Médico Costamed at your request, Costamed may handle that information under its own policies. You may also need to contact Costamed directly regarding records it maintains.
