Regenerative Medicine

Stem Cells and Exosomes

Mesenchymal stem cells & exosomes derivated from donated human placental mesenchymal stem cells can exert anti-aging effects by promoting tissue repair and regeneration, modulating inflammation and immune responses, regulating cellular senescence, enhancing angiogenesis, protecting against oxidative stress and cell death, and modulating age-related epigenetic changes.

Stem cell therapy

The stem cell therapy we provide is an allogenic transplant (from a donor).

Stem cell therapy offers promising possibilities in regenerative medicine, with potential applications in supporting tissue repair and recovery across various medical conditions. Treatment outcomes and response times may vary from patient to patient.

Exosome therapy

Exosomes are not cells but microvesicles that contain proteins, microRNA, and growth factors.

Exosome therapy is an emerging area of regenerative medicine, with its suitability and potential benefits depending on several factors, including the patient's overall health and individual needs. The source and quality of exosomes are carefully evaluated as part of the treatment process.

Treatment plans and outcomes may vary from person to person. While some individuals may be considered for one or two sessions, others may require additional sessions based on their condition and the specialist's assessment. The duration and extent of any potential benefits cannot be guaranteed.

The applications of Stem cells & MSC exosomes for body aging treatments include:

Skin Rejuvenation
Osteoarthritis and Joint Health
Muscle Regeneration
Neurodegenerative Diseases
Cardiovascular Health
Metabolic Health
